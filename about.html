My path through the Visual Arts has been expressed in many forms: from creating sets for theater, film and television, motion graphics, art animations and visual effects, to sculptures and collages.

In a professional environment or when teaching a class, I am always committed to being creative and giving all my knowledge and my soul.

About Me

I was born in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Art classes and two years of architectural studies were kept aside and I moved to Brazil. Colors and rhythms inspired my life as a set designer in cinema, theater and television.

An awarded scholarship brought me to New York City where I began creating virtual sets, motion graphics, and art animations while continuing to develop sculptures, collages, and mosaics.

I rebuild moments, memories, words, photographs, videos and music from my culturally diverse background to compose the palette of my artwork, which I call “In Pieces.”

Teaching & Professional Background

My career combines motion graphics, animation, visual effects,fine arts and set design for television, film and theater.

Since 2011, I have shared my experience through teaching Motion Graphics, Compositing, Visiual Effects and Digital Media Arts at BMCC, BCC and York CUNY colleges and previously at St. John's University.
